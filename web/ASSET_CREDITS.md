## 2. Character & Animation System
- **Implementation:** Procedural Articulated Humanoid Rigging & Locomotion Controller (`WorkerCharacter.tsx`)
- **Proportions:** Calibrated to real-world adult humanoid scale (~1.74m total height, 0.46m seated pelvis height, 0.74m desk typing position).
- **Styling:** Contemporary Scandinavian office casual & smart techwear (tailored chinos, collared shirts, knitwear, spectacles, styled hair).
- **License:** Original procedural code developed for Punggawa, MIT compatible.
